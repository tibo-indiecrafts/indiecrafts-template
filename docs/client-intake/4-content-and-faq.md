# Page content & FAQ — fill-in form

The full text of each page feeds both your website **and** the detailed version
AI assistants read (`llms-full`). You don't need to re-supply page copy here —
it comes in with your page designs. This form is for the **FAQ**, which is worth
its own attention:

- In Google it can win an expandable **"People also ask"–style rich result**.
- It's exactly the kind of Q&A that AI assistants quote back to users.
- The same answers appear on the page, in the search markup, and in the AI
  files — you write them once per language.

> 🌐 **Languages:** your FAQ appears in each of your site's languages (English +
> French by default) — a French visitor sees the French FAQ. Please give every
> question and answer in **all** your site's languages. Easiest: write the
> translation right under each original Q/A.
