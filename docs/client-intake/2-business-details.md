# Business details — fill-in form

This powers the "business card" Google can show next to your site (name,
address, opening hours, area served) and helps AI assistants describe you
accurately. Fill in whatever applies — anything left blank is simply left out,
nothing breaks.

> 🌐 **Languages:** most of this is the same in every language — address, phone,
> hours, coordinates, price, and links don't change. The only field to give in
> **each** of your site's languages (English + French by default) is any
> free-text description. Everything else you fill in once.
